Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information ReceiptGenPro (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects when you use this website, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The Receipt Generator Runs in Your Browser

The core receipt-generator tool on this site is client-side: when you enter a business name, customer name, item, price, or upload a logo, that information is processed directly in your browser to render and export your receipt. It is not transmitted to or stored on our servers. If you close the browser tab without downloading, that data is gone — we never had a copy of it.

Information We Do Collect

Like most websites, we (and the third-party services we use, listed below) automatically collect standard technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and referring URL — typically through cookies and similar technologies, for analytics and advertising purposes.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies to operate and improve this site, understand how visitors use it, and (where enabled) to serve advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect how parts of this site function.

Advertising and Third-Party Vendors

This site may display advertisements served by Google and other third-party advertising vendors. These vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the Google DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

At the moment no advertising network or analytics service is active on this site. If we enable Google AdSense or Google Analytics, we will name them here and they will only load after you accept cookies.

Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use this site in aggregate. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to collect and analyze information about site usage.

Your Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the UK, you generally have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (including for advertising). Contact us using the details on our Contact page to exercise these rights.

Your Rights (California / CCPA-CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to request deletion, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (which can include certain advertising cookie practices). Contact us to make a request.

Children’s Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention

Because the receipt generator itself processes data client-side, we do not retain the content of receipts you create. Standard technical/analytics data collected by third-party services is retained according to those services’ own retention policies.
