What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site owners and their advertising/analytics partners.

Cookies We Would Use

Today our own code sets no cookies. The table shows the kinds of cookies the site would use if we turn on analytics or advertising; a banner will ask for your consent before any of them is set.

Category Purpose
Essential Basic site functionality (e.g. remembering that you accepted this cookie notice)
Analytics Understanding how visitors use the site, in aggregate, to improve it
Advertising Serving and measuring ads, including personalized advertising based on prior visits to this and other sites

At the moment this site does not run analytics or advertising scripts. If we add Google Analytics or Google AdSense, they will only load after you accept cookies, and we will list the specific cookies they set on this page. Google explains its own cookies at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google specifically via Google’s Ads Settings, and from participating vendors generally via www.aboutads.info. Note that blocking some cookies may impact your experience of this site.

What This Site Stores Today

The receipt generator uses no cookies. To save you retyping, it keeps a few things in your browser’s own storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to us:

  • rgp-design (localStorage): the receipt design you picked last.
  • rgp-last-receipt-number (localStorage): the number of the last receipt you downloaded, so the next one continues from it.
  • rgp-form:<page address> (sessionStorage): the form you are filling in, and your logo, so a reload doesn’t lose them. It is deleted when you close the tab.
  • rgp-business-profile (localStorage, only if you tick “Remember my business details on this device”): your business name, address, contact details, tax number, logo and currency, so your next receipt starts with them on any page of the generator. It stays on your device until you click “Forget saved details”, untick the box, or clear this site’s data in your browser, and it is never uploaded.

You can clear them at any time in your browser’s site settings.
