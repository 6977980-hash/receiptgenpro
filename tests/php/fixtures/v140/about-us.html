ReceiptGenPro is a free online receipt generator built for freelancers, landlords, small retailers and service businesses who need a professional, itemized receipt in seconds — no signup, no watermark, and no cost.

What We Built

We were tired of receipt tools that force a signup just to download a single PDF, stamp a watermark across it unless you pay, or bury the actual generator behind ads and upsells. ReceiptGenPro does the opposite: fill in a form, watch your receipt build itself in real time, and download it — that’s the whole product.

How It Works

The receipt generator runs entirely in your browser. When you type a customer’s name or an item price, that data is used only to render the receipt on your screen — it is never uploaded to our servers. You can read exactly how in our Privacy Policy.

What We Never Store

There are no accounts on ReceiptGenPro, so there is nothing to sign up for and no receipt history kept on our side. Here is what happens to what you type:

  • Your receipt details (names, items, prices, notes) are used only to draw the preview in your browser. They are never sent to our server.
  • Your logo is read by your browser and shown in the preview. It is not uploaded.
  • A reload doesn’t lose your work: the form, and your logo, are kept in your browser tab’s session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Your downloads (PDF, PNG or JPEG) and the receipt’s QR code are created on your own device, with three open-source libraries from the cdnjs CDN. qrcode-generator loads with the generator while “Add a verification QR code” is ticked (it is by default) and draws the code in your browser. html2canvas and jsPDF load only when you click a download button, and build the file locally. We never see or keep a copy.
